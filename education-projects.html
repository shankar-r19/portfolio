04

Acting Out

Often in classrooms and settings where learning happens, students go through multiple cognitive states and loads during the process of learning, “Acting Out” captures their cognitive states and impulses. The project makes use of origami to signify cognitive load and an iris mechanism to symbolize multiple types of cognitive load. The object seems to wander and requires an empathetic embrace by the user to go back to state of low cognitive load.
